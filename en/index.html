A personal secretary on your phone

You keep the calendar. Meira gets you there.

Meira reads your calendar and works backwards from every meeting. She decides when you wake up, how long you need to get ready, when to leave and which way to go. Then she tells you each one at the right moment.

How to install
  1. Tap Download for Android and open Meira.apk.
  2. If Android asks, allow installs from this source (your browser).
  3. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details → Install anyway. On Samsung, if the install is blocked: Settings → Security and privacy → Auto Blocker → off.
  4. When you connect Google Calendar, Google says the app isn't verified yet. That's because we're in beta: tap Advanced, then go to the app.

How it works

You add the meeting. Meira handles the rest.

Put it in your calendar

As you already do. Meira picks up new events on her own, including the ones a recruiter sends with the address hidden in the invitation text.

She works backwards

From the start time she counts back through the trip, the time you need to get ready and anything you should prepare, all the way to your alarm.

She gets you out the door

The alarm rings, the "leave now" arrives on time, and if you're still home when the bus is close, she tells you that too.

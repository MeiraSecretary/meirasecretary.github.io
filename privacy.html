מה זו מאירה

מאירה היא עוזרת אישית לתכנון היום. היא קוראת את היומן שלכם, מחשבת מתי צריך לקום ולצאת כדי להגיע בזמן, ומאפשרת לדבר איתה על היום שלכם באפליקציה או בוואטסאפ.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026 · App: Meira for Android (com.lumi.app) · Contact: koralzakai1@gmail.com

What Meira is

Meira is a personal day-planning assistant. She reads your calendar, works out when you need to wake up and leave to arrive on time, and lets you talk to her about your day in the app or on WhatsApp.

We only collect what a feature needs. If you never use a feature, we never collect its data.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers. If you connect an AI assistant (for example Claude or ChatGPT) with a personal key from the app, that assistant receives only what it asks Meira for, and you can revoke the key at any time.

Google user data

Meira's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide Meira's planning features to you. It is never used for advertising, never sold, never used to train AI models, and a person reads it only with your consent, for security, or where the law requires.

How your data is protected

  • Every record is tied to your account and the database enforces it: one user cannot read another user's rows.
  • The server requires a verified sign-in token on every request, and identifies you only from that token.
  • Personal data on the server is encrypted at rest, and all traffic is HTTPS.
  • Your Google access stays on your phone, in encrypted storage.

No system is perfect, and we will not claim otherwise. If something goes wrong, we will tell the users affected.

How long we keep it

While your account exists. Voice recordings and email text are not kept at all.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete the data we hold about you by emailing koralzakai1@gmail.com.

Deleting your account: in the app, 👤 → Delete my account, or by email. See Delete your account. Everything is deleted within 30 days.

You can revoke any permission (location, microphone, contacts, calendar) in Android settings. Meira keeps working; only the feature that needs it stops.

Children

Meira is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Where your data is processed

Our server and database are hosted in the European Union. Some providers (speech-to-text and web search) process data in the United States.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
